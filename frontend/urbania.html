Force Urbania

Luxury Travel, Built For Groups.

The Force Urbania is a European-styled monocoque luxury van that redefines group travel in India. Unlike traditional ladder-frame Tempo Travellers, its car-like monocoque construction delivers a lower floor, a smoother ride, better cabin insulation, and superior safety, so a full day on the road feels effortless.

With a high roof you can stand in, wide captain-style seating, panoramic windows, and a quiet, refined cabin, the Urbania is the natural choice for family holidays to Coorg and Chikmagalur, corporate offsites, airport transfers for teams, wedding logistics, and multi-day outstation tours from Bengaluru.

Every RYDEXS Urbania is meticulously maintained, fully air-conditioned, and driven by professional, route-experienced chauffeurs, so your group simply relaxes and enjoys the journey.

ON BOARD

Comfort In Every Detail.

The Urbania is loaded with amenities that keep long journeys relaxed and enjoyable for the whole group.

  • Push-Back Recliner Seats: Wide, individually reclining captain seats with generous legroom and armrests for genuine long-haul comfort.
  • Powerful Dual AC: Front and roof-mounted air-conditioning cools the entire cabin evenly, even in peak summer traffic.
  • Ample Luggage Space: A dedicated rear boot plus overhead cabins comfortably swallow suitcases for the whole travelling group.
  • USB & Charging Points: Multiple charging outlets keep phones, cameras and laptops powered on the move.
  • Safety First: ABS, dual airbags, monocoque crash structure and 3-point seat belts across the cabin.
  • Quiet, Refined Cabin: Superior insulation and a smooth ride let conversation and music flow, no shouting over road noise.
