Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Last updated: 19 September 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you enquire about or book a trip with RYDEXS, we may collect information such as your name, phone number, email address, travel date, destination, traveller count, and any other details you share with us through the website, trip planner, WhatsApp, or phone.

We may also collect limited technical information — such as browser type, device, and pages visited — to help us understand how our website is used.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To confirm and manage your booking, including coordinating vehicles and drivers.
  • To contact you regarding your enquiry, trip details, or booking assistance.
  • To respond to questions submitted through our contact form or trip planner.
  • To improve our services, website, and the overall travel experience.
  • To send trip-related updates, such as confirmations or driver details.

3. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. Your details are shared only with the driver and vehicle partner assigned to your trip, to the extent necessary to fulfil your booking, or with service providers who help us operate our booking and communication systems (such as our website hosting and messaging tools).

We may also disclose information where required by law or to protect the safety of our passengers, drivers, or the public.

4. Cookies & Tracking

Our website may use basic cookies or local storage to remember preferences such as dark mode, and to understand general usage patterns. These do not identify you personally and can be cleared through your browser settings at any time.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information from unauthorised access, loss, or misuse. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we encourage you to share only the information necessary for your booking.

6. Your Rights & Choices

You may ask us to review, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you by contacting us using the details below. You may also opt out of promotional communications at any time; we will still need to contact you regarding an active booking.

7. Data Retention

We retain booking and enquiry information for as long as needed to provide our services, meet legal or accounting obligations, and resolve any disputes, after which it is deleted or anonymised.

8. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for use by adults booking travel. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or applicable law. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised "last updated" date.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, reach out to us at +91 99642 99742, email support@rydexs.in, or visit our Contact page.
